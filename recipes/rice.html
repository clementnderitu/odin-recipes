Vegetable Fried Rice

Description:

Vegetable fried rice is a simple and colorful dish made with cooked rice and mixed vegetables. It is quick to prepare and can be served as a main meal or as a side dish.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1/2 cup sweet corn
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste
  • Spring onions, chopped

Steps:

  1. Heat cooking oil in a large pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the chopped onion and garlic, then fry until fragrant.
  3. Add the carrots, peas, and sweet corn.
  4. Stir-fry the vegetables for 3–4 minutes until slightly soft.
  5. Add the cooked rice and mix well with the vegetables.
  6. Add soy sauce, salt, and black pepper.
  7. Stir-fry for another 3–5 minutes.
  8. Garnish with spring onions and serve hot.
