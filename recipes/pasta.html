Creamy Garlic Pasta

Description:

Creamy garlic pasta is a rich and comforting meal made with pasta, garlic, milk, and cream. It is easy to make and perfect for lunch or dinner.

Ingredients:

  • 250g pasta
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup cooking cream
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley, chopped

Steps:

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until soft.
  2. Drain the pasta and set it aside.
  3. Melt butter in a pan over medium heat.
  4. Add minced garlic and cook for about 1 minute.
  5. Add milk and cooking cream, then stir well.
  6. Add grated cheese and stir until the sauce becomes creamy.
  7. Season with salt and black pepper.
  8. Add the cooked pasta and mix until fully coated.
  9. Garnish with parsley and serve warm.
