Banana Pancake

Description:

Banana pancakes are soft, sweet, and easy to prepare using ripe bananas. They are perfect for breakfast and can be served with honey, syrup, or fruit.

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil or butter
  • Honey or syrup for serving

Steps:

  1. Peel the bananas and mash them in a bowl.
  2. Add the egg and milk, then mix well.
  3. Add flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  4. Stir until a smooth batter forms.
  5. Heat a pan and lightly grease it with oil or butter.
  6. Pour a small amount of batter into the pan.
  7. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface, then flip the pancake.
  8. Cook the other side until golden brown.
  9. Serve with honey, syrup, or fresh fruit.
